TACTICAL SPECIFICATIONS v1.6.0
🚀 HYPERSPACE & SOLAR NAVIGATION CONTROLS
W / UP ARROW: Fire main thrusters / Accelerate forward.
A / LEFT ARROW: Rotate ship nose left.
D / RIGHT ARROW: Rotate ship nose right.
SHIFT: Engage Impulse Overdrive for high-speed evasion boost.
ENTER: Enter Star System or Planet Orbit when near orbital marker.
S: Short-range scan. Fully identifies and logs every contact within sensor range.
SHIFT + S: Long-range sweep. Paints distant objects as ? unidentified contacts on the Star Map - you must close to short range and scan again to identify them. Recharges for 6 seconds.
SENSOR RANGE scales with your Navigator's skill and the Scanner module fitted at the Depot. A rookie navigator with a Standard Scanner reaches ~41 LY short / ~116 LY long; an expert with a Mineral Resonance Scan reaches ~97 LY / ~273 LY.
B: Board a derelict station or salvage an alien wreck when in range.
E: Investigate a subspace distress beacon.
W: Enter a quantum wormhole. The LAND control also performs whichever of these is in range.
ARCHIVES: Starbase Prime keeps a Central Archive, and three alien starports run their own - the Thrynn Trade Reliquary, the Veloxi Imperial Codex and the Spemin Collected Wisdoms. Dock with [B] and read. Some volumes are sealed until you have recovered enough artifacts or read the prerequisite text. Anything actionable is filed automatically under CLUES in the Captain's Log [G].
CUSTOMS PATROLS: Starbase Prime cutters sweep the core sectors within 130 LY of the base and hail passing ships for a cargo scan. Carrying Spemin Spice in an unshielded hold means seizure plus a 500 M.U. per-unit fine. Shielded Cargo Bays (cargo class 3) defeat the scan outright. You may also jettison the cargo before they sweep, or run - evasion odds improve with engine class. Outside their jurisdiction, nobody is checking.
L: Dock at Starbase Prime (at 100, 100) or launch Terrain Lander on planet surface.
M: Toggle Fullscreen Interactive Star Map with zoom & pan.
G: Open Captain's Exploration Log & Survey History Archive.
?: Toggle this Command Manual.
🪐 PLANETARY SURFACE EXPLORATION & MINING
ARROW KEYS / WASD: Drive Terrain Vehicle (TV) Rover across 50x35 sector planetary grid.
A / ENTER: Analyze mineral/bio deposit under Rover or harvest items into cargo.
I / U AT LANDER: Inspect TV cargo hold or unload cargo samples into Lander storage.
L AT LANDER: Ascend back into space orbit.
TELEMETRY HUD: Real-time compass heading (↗ NE, ➡ E, etc.), sector distance to lander pad [H], required return power, and safety margin status (SAFE / WARNING / CRITICAL).
⚔️ REAL-TIME TACTICAL COMBAT & SHIELDS
F: Fire Phaser Blasters (green directional lasers).
V: Launch Homing Missiles (high damage explosive ordnance).
K: Toggle Deflector Shields (absorbs incoming alien plasma bolts).
ARM WEAPONS (control bar): Arms or safes the cannon banks. Weapons also arm automatically on launch when AUTO-ARM WEAPONS is enabled at the Depot.
SHIFT: Evasive impulse roll to dodge enemy plasma torpedoes.
C: Open Subspace Comms Dialogue with alien vessels at any time.
